Returns Policy

Our standard guidelines for returns and refunds.

Last updated: September 2026

1. Our Returns Commitment

At StanMatt, we want you to receive products that meet your expectations. If an eligible product arrives damaged, defective or incorrect, you may request a return or replacement.

2. Eligible Returns

Products may generally be eligible for return when they are unused, in their original condition and packaging, and accompanied by proof of purchase.

3. Damaged or Incorrect Items

If you receive a damaged, defective or incorrect item, please contact customer care as soon as possible after delivery and provide your order details.

4. Groceries and Perishable Products

For health and safety reasons, perishable groceries may have different return conditions. Damaged, spoiled or incorrectly delivered grocery items should be reported promptly.

5. Refunds

Approved refunds will be processed using the applicable payment method. Processing times may vary depending on the payment provider.

6. Non-Returnable Items

Certain products may not be eligible for return because of hygiene, safety, perishability or other applicable restrictions.

7. How to Request a Return

Contact StanMatt customer care with your order number, the product concerned and a description of the issue. Photographs may be requested for damaged products.

8. Policy Changes

StanMatt may update this policy from time to time. The latest version will be published on this page.
