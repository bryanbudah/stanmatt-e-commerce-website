Frequently Asked Questions

How do I place an order?

Browse our products, add the items you want to your cart, proceed to checkout and follow the instructions to complete your order.

What payment methods do you accept?

StanMatt supports secure payment options including M-Pesa and card payments.

How long does delivery take?

Delivery times depend on your location and the availability of your selected products. Estimated delivery information will be provided during checkout.

Can I cancel my order?

Orders may be cancelled before they enter the delivery process. Contact our customer care team as soon as possible.

Still need help?

Contact our customer care team and we'll be happy to assist.
